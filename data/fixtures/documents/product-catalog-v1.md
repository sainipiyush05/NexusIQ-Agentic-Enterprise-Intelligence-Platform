# AtlasCloud Product Catalog

## Product codes
AC-ST is the Starter plan at 29 USD per month. AC-GR is the Growth plan at 99 USD per month. AC-EN is the Enterprise plan at 299 USD per month. Prices exclude taxes. These product codes are global across APAC, EMEA and AMER.
## Limits
Starter includes 5 seats. Growth includes 25 seats. Enterprise includes 100 seats. Additional seats require a separate agreement.
